### Owner specification — Customer Plan Entry page / SKU-level effective plan (2026-10-14)
- One customer Plan Entry page under Procurement, selection header = authorised company, plan month, plan type (Rolling / Final / Additional / Urgent). Under it display company SKU list in existing **ErpDenseGrid** (dense keyboard-first), not cards or side panels. Owner will approve exact columns and layout later.
- Rolling usually arrives by ~20th of preceding month for a three-month horizon, e.g. Oct/Nov/Dec rolling received 20 Sep. Rolling for future Nov/Dec remains user-editable; edits are versioned, not destructive.
- Final arrives later (e.g. October Final on 5 October), and **replaces Rolling only for the SKU×company×month keys actually provided as Final**. Other SKUs/months continue to use their Rolling values. Retain superseded rolling in history; effective base is latest Final if present else latest Rolling, not sum of both.
- Additional demand adds to the effective base (Rolling OR Final) **per SKU/company/month**. Preserve individual additional entries/history. Do not add replaced Rolling to Final.
- Urgent supports repeated independent entries even weekly for same SKU/month. On Urgent mode ask **From Existing Plan** vs **Extra Urgent** plus required From/To period: From Plan accelerates timing of allocated existing demand, *without increasing total*; Extra Urgent increases total demand and has its own required period. Preserve every entry and prevent duplicate demand. For From Plan ensure allocation cannot overdraw the remaining unallocated existing base/additional demand; implementation must define partial overlap and period validation without inventing business restrictions.
- Effective monthly demand concept: latest Final if present else latest Rolling, plus Additional, plus Extra Urgent. Urgent From Plan is a dated scheduling constraint *within* that quantity, not another addition. Historical plan source preserved.
- Plan entry/edit actions extend beyond Procurement to appropriately authorised **Plant Managers and Stores & Logistics** users, company-scoped and action-scoped. Record who changed what and when, including revisions; no plan-approval workflow.
- Do not fold stroke/producing-company allocation, AI Procurement suggestions or material planning into this Plan Entry page without a later explicit page-specific owner choice. No implementation or PROD write authorised by this documentation.
